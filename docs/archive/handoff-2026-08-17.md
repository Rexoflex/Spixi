## What the audit found — 4 MAJORs in the +257 lines

Full write-up in `DECISIONS.md` #340. The short version, worst first:

1. **A peer's nickname could blank your payment-confirm screen.** The `sendUiCommand`
   data-URI fast path was gated on the shape of the VALUE, and "round-trip identical"
   assumed every receiver runs `native.js`. The 8 remaining legacy `Raw/html` pages decode
   with an unguarded `atob`, which throws on the `:` — dropping the whole push. Fixed by
   gating on the RECEIVER (`supportsRawDataUriArgs`, fails closed). Same bug pointed at
   MiniAppPage, whose decoder ships inside third-party packages and can never be regenerated.
2. **The password pane could park itself over the next tab.** `closeSublevelOverlays()` only
   swept `overlayStack`, and `pushPageLoaded` is load-then-present — for the whole boot
   window the page is in `activePreload` with its stage already parented to HomePage, and
   nothing cancels it when its opener dies. That window is exactly when the user leaves,
   because the screen deliberately looks unchanged while it loads. Round 2 widened the sweep
   to all three sublevels the Account stages (mobile Downloads/Backup had the same strand).
3. **The apps perf latch removed the self-heal.** `loadApps` runs on two threads with no
   marshalling; interleaved, one run's `clearApps` drops rows the other delivered, and the
   latch made the short list stick for the session. Now serialized, latched after the push,
   and cleared when the document dies rather than when the new one announces itself.
4. **`ixian:startappwith:` could invite people to an app that does not exist** — app ids are
   not charset-validated at install, so an id containing the `:|` delimiter mis-splits. Now
   rejected before any invite is sent.
