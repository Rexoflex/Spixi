Spixi Thread

illustration + motion language · proposal

Spixi has no server in the middle: a message goes along one direct line from you to them. Thread makes that line the whole illustration language. Every drawing is one ink line with two flat colour planes printed slightly off-register, and a single spark that travels the line. It replaces the glossy tiles everywhere, in light and dark.

Swipe the card slowly: the thread leaves one scene and enters the next at the same height, so the six scenes read as one line. No parallax on purpose.

The language · five rules

01 · LINEOne ink line draws everything

2.2 u, round caps and joins, the theme ink. No fills of its own. It enters every scene at the left edge and leaves at the right edge at the same height.

02 · PLANESTwo flat planes, off-register

A soft tint and one brand accent, printed 6 u right and down from the line, like a two-colour print. No gradients, gloss, glow or shadow.

03 · SPARKOne spark travels the line

The message on its way: a 4 u dot runs the thread every 5 s (0.2 Hz). The only loop in the language; it waits while the card is off screen.

04 · GROUNDA tinted panel on a dot grid

The network, quietly: a 14 u dot grid at 9 % on a brand-tinted ground. The full-bleed hero inverts onto brand indigo with a white line.

05 · MOTIONDraw, settle, pop, hold

The line draws itself (0.5–1.2 s), the planes slide 6 u into their offset, small nodes pop, then the frame holds. Reduced motion shows the end frame without the spark.
