Session U — the seven queued dials, and the two that were already done (#845-#857)

Damir asked to close the FE work. Every dial arrived with its mechanism recorded, so the
job was to verify the mechanism still held (#215) and build — not to re-investigate. Two of
the seven had already moved: AND-35's copy and order change is BUILT and has been since
#774/#371, and #839's "no button and no chevron" was half wrong — the chevron has been there
since #249, and the defect was that its gate could never be true. Both were caught by reading
the tree first.

#846 · #838 — the Add-app screen sat at 32px from each edge. #560 fixed this exact defect
once already, and its own comment states the invariant ("a component hosted in this body does
not pay the inset twice") while enforcing it as a list of the two components mounted that day.
#827 mounted a third. The selector is the invariant now, and GATE 57 derives the mounted set
from what home.html actually appends.

#847 · #841 — a long member nick carried its remove x off the group-create card. Fixed in
chip.css so every host benefits, with capping as the default and horizontal scrollers opting
out. GATE 58's first draft checked one direction; the second found a real miss — a
chip-holding scroller with no opt-out that the new default had silently capped.

#848 · #837 — the Requests chip loses its digit, and the same number still decides whether
the chip exists. M5 was re-read as #837 required: four of five clauses always passed, and the
failing one asserted a spelling iOS-26 moved while the feature stayed live. M5 is green and
leaves KNOWN_PREEXISTERS — the baseline is 2.

#849 · #842 — "Vas Spixi ID" on somebody else's contact page. English is neutral, which is
why it survived: no reviewer of the English could see it. Fixed by splitting the key, not by
re-wording three locales, because the ambiguity is the defect. The new key is held out of
build-locales' legacy reuse, or its identical English would map it straight back to the value
that IS the defect.

#850 · #839 — the member sheet had nowhere to go: onViewContact was declared, read, invoked
and passed by no production host. Both halves ship (the handler and a visible button), plus
the address-keyed verb the host did not have. The verb is a LOOKUP and nothing else.

#851 · #840 — an app you already have was offered as a fresh install, because the screen was
told what the route wanted rather than what the store said.

#852 · #836 — Add app and Add contact return to the desktop pane, as a fork rather than a
revert: the cold boot is what makes the page push wrong on a phone, the detail column is what
makes it right on a desktop. It cannot key on data-desktop (a constant across resize), so C#
publishes the live pane state. A mutation deleting the resize push left every clause green,
which is why the gate grew a clause for the C# half.

#853 · #835 — Doodles and Live flow retired, chat-flow.js deleted. The deletion was the small
half: fifteen existing pins had their subject removed, one of which still read the deleted
module and would have thrown and killed the suite. A retirement is a fall-through, never a
migration, and there are four readers, not three.

#855 - the gradient option retired, not just defaulted away from. #853 shipped the default
flip and asked which reading he meant, because his 2026-09-09 words sat against his own
2026-08-30 ruling; he answered "retire the option for now". CHAT_GROUNDS drops to one member,
which hides the Colour row through a DERIVED guard rather than a switch, and all three ladders
drop the value from their allowlist - a user who PICKED Gradient must not be stranded on a
canvas the picker can no longer change. "For now" shaped the rest: the token, the CSS and the
plumbing stay, and restoring the option is one member.

#856 - the icon re-export is deliberate: Damir is testing THINNER icons. Logged, not folded in
- and the finding exposed a real gap: nothing compares the SVG sources with the generated
registry, and generate-icons.mjs has no --check, so the two can disagree with the suite green.
