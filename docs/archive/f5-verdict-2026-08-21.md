## 3. The nine findings

### F1 🔴 A WHITE FLASH before the lock, everywhere
Launch, every background with the lock on, and always before the pattern screen. It is
**not app content** — the exposure is closed — but it is ugly and it is on the security
surface. Same family as the C3.1 report that never reproduced.
**Not diagnosed. Candidates, in order:** the Android window background (`MainTheme`) is
light and shows between the modal push and the lock's first paint · the modal push
transition paints the window ground · `lock.html`'s instant-bg (#203) is not reaching the
first frame on this path. ⚠ Do not guess — it is one screenshot-timing or one log line away.

### F2 🟡 The lock's status bar is SPLASH-BLUE, not the lock's dark
`repaintSystemBarsFor` is called on the LockPage F-4 paths and on `dismissPauseLock`, but
apparently not for the **pause-presented** lock. Likely one missing call. Related to F1 —
both are "the lock's ground is not owning the whole window on this path".

### F3 🔴 NO biometric / pattern prompt on resume — TWO FAILED FIXES
★ **The next step is INSTRUMENTATION, not a third attempt.** History:
* #454 audit MAJOR-2 guarded on `App.isInForeground` → did nothing: that flag is cleared
  in `OnSleep`, which MAUI raises from Android's **OnStop**, one step after the `OnPause`
  where the pause lock is created.
* #460 replaced it with an explicit latch (`deferAuthentication` / `onForegroundReturned`).
  **Still fails on device.**

Cold start DOES prompt (1.12 / A7 pass), so the plugin and the enrolment are fine — it is
the RESUME path only. Log all four gates and the branch:
`uiReady` · `pageVisible` · `authAttempted` · `authDeferred`, plus whether
`App.OnResume` reached `held.onForegroundReturned()` at all.
⚠ Note Damir's device uses a **PATTERN**, i.e. the device-credential fallback
(`AllowAlternativeAuthentication = true`), not a fingerprint. Whether androidx treats that
path differently after a pause is an open question and a candidate cause.

### F4 🔴 The Close button on the FULL-SCREEN tx page does nothing — ROOT-CAUSED
★ **This is a regression I introduced in #453 ④.** `wallet_sent.html:307-326` LIFTS the
`.c-txsheet` card out of a ghost sheet and reparents it into the page. The Close button
added at `src/components/wallet-shell.js:620-626` rides along with it and calls
`closeSheet(sheet)` on a sheet that was closed and discarded lines earlier — so it renders
on a surface it cannot act on.
**Damir's steer, and it is right: it belongs only in the SHEET.** Fix: an explicit
`showClose` option on `openTxSheet`, default true, passed `false` by `wallet_sent.html`
beside its existing `disclose: false`. Do NOT key it off `disclose` — they are different
questions and coupling them is how the next surface inherits the wrong one.

### F5 🔴 The wallet zero state must have NO glyph either — ROOT-CAUSED
`src/components/wallet-shell.js:104` — `glyph: 'wallet'`. #453 ⑥ dropped the illustration
and left the tile standing in. Damir: *"THE ICON MUST BE REMOVED, there's no glyph or
illustration on wallet activity empty state."* One line. ⚠ Check `createEmptyState`
renders correctly with neither an illustration nor a glyph before assuming it is free.

### F6 🟡 The scan row appears and disappears at random
Damir, on 5.1. The anchor itself looks right across a restart. Suspects, both in
`src/components/scan-progress.js:164-224`: the SHOW_LAG 20 / HIDE_LAG 2 hysteresis, and
the indeterminate (`target == 0`, no peers) state un-hiding the row — which is exactly the
#446 MAJOR shape, where hysteresis keyed on visibility was reopened by the indeterminate
frame. Needs the pushed `current`/`target`/`origin` triple logged over ~60 s.

### F7 🟡 "Show amounts" is not displayed on Android
`wallet_sent.html:258` — `canReveal()` is `hideKnown && walletHidden && !revealed`.
`WalletSentPage.xaml.cs:97` pushes `setHideBalance`. Cannot be decided from source: log
which of the two flags is false when the page renders. Adjacent to the known pre-exister
**B3**.

### F8 🔴 One ACCOUNT, and wiping it must wipe clean — DAMIR'S PRODUCT CALL
Creating a new account after deleting the wallet said *"there's an existing account on
this device"*, and the new account then **inherited the old one**. Damir's steer, verbatim:
*"just make it an account, and if you wipe it it wipes the app clean of any account/wallet
file, you start clean unless you restore."*
This is **N67** and it now has its design decision. ⚠ Destructive-path work: the
delete→restore error must be reproduced and named FIRST (#215/#294), it needs a
security-gate row, and it must not strand a live wallet behind onboarding (the W14/#348
guard).

### F9 🔴 A fresh account shows the OLD balance, and "Connecting…" never completes
The stale balance clears on an app restart, so it is an echo — a live `setBalance` push
surviving the account swap, or `IxianHandler.balances` not being cleared. The persisting
"Connecting…" is **N69** confirmed a THIRD time (post-creation connect never completes
until restart). N69(a) is Core-side; the balance echo is probably ours and is separable.
