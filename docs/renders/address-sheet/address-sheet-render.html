Spixi Address Sheet

The “Your Ixian address” sheet, opened from Account by tapping the address or QR in the header. Damir: it “seems a bit out of place with our premium app feel”. Today’s sheet is a stack of four boxes (grey info, white QR, grey address, green safety) with three tints and two icon-only actions. Below: a faithful redraw of today, then two premium directions, each in light and dark, plus one frame showing the state that matters (copy feedback, full address).

The code in every frame is a real, scannable QR of the payload the app encodes today (address + ":ixi"), built with the app’s own encoder. Taps work: Copy shows its confirmation, “Show all” opens the full address.

Why it reads as out of place

  • Four boxes, three tints. Grey info, white QR, grey address, green safety. Every part has the same weight, so nothing reads as the hero.
  • Two paragraphs of explanation (5 lines) for a screen whose job is “show the code”.
  • The address is unverifiable at a glance: 65 characters wrapped by width (3 ragged lines), no grouping.
  • No primary action. Share and Copy are two 18 px icons with no label; nothing says whose code this is.
Direction A

Identity card

Same sheet, same height class as today. Everything sits on one premium card: a soft band in the illustration kit’s halo colours, your avatar cut into it, your name, the code on a white plate, and the address in five-character groups with Copy right under it. Share is the one filled button.

What changed and why

  • One card instead of four boxes. The band, the avatar ring and the white plate give it depth; the rest of the sheet is quiet. Reads like the About header and the What’s new card.
  • Identity on the code. Avatar and name tell the person scanning whose code it is (and tell you that you opened your own).
  • A verifiable address. 13 groups of 5 in monospace, 3 tidy rows. The first and last group are bold: the two people actually compare.
  • Share is the primary action (full-width, labelled). Copy is a labelled pill next to the value it copies, and it confirms in place: “Copied” with a green check for 1.4 s, announced to screen readers.
  • Explanation and safety are one line each. “Scan to add me or send me IXI.” under the name; “Safe to share. It can’t open your wallet.” at the foot, no box.

Cost and risk LOW · ~1 day

  • wallet-receive.js · openAddressSheet only: the two explainRow blocks, the caption and the addrRow chip are replaced by one card node. createQrSvg, copyText, the copyMorph timing and the live region are kept as they are.
  • Two new optional params, no bridge verb: { name, avatar } from the caller (Account already shows both in its header; the wallet Receive caller would pass the same profile). createAvatar is already imported. Share uses the existing onShare → ixian:share.
  • Peer subject keeps its fences: the contact’s avatar and name, Copy only (no Share, the !peer fence), no safety line.
  • Strings: 2 new keys (card sub line, short safety line); copy, txCopied, shareAddress exist. The band is CSS only.
  • Risk: the smoke pins on .c-addr-sheet__info ×2, __explain--safe and __sharebtn must move to the new classes. #575 retired a full-width outline Share as “a too-short bar”; this one is the filled primary, which needs Damir’s OK.
Direction B

Glass hero

A full-height sheet in the About header family: the launcher-blue band with its light fields, sheen and rings, your avatar and name in white, and the code floating on a white plate across the band’s edge. The address is shown as start … end with “Show all”. Copy and Share are docked at the bottom, in thumb reach.

What changed and why

  • The code is the hero, literally. The largest code of the three (232 px), on a white plate that floats over the band, with the About rings opening round it. Made to be held up and scanned.
  • Short address by default, full on demand. 4JsLS mAF4y ··· g5yYb ncdFa is what people check; “Show all” opens the 13 groups of 5 in place.
  • Docked action pair. Copy (tonal) and Share (filled, primary), the bottom-bar grammar of the wallet. Copy morphs to “Copied ✓”.
  • Request an amount as one quiet row: it opens the wallet’s existing Receive flow (pick contacts, then the keypad). Leave it out if Account should stay single-purpose.
  • The safety line sits above the buttons, right where the decision to share is made, as one line.

Cost and risk MEDIUM · ~2–3 days

  • Sheet chrome changes. createSheet paints its own handle and c-sheet__title; the hero needs them white over the band (a c-sheet--hero modifier, or the title moved into the hero with aria-labelledby kept). That touches sheet.js/overlay CSS, which every sheet shares.
  • New layout in openAddressSheet: hero node (CSS art, reuses --ab-band-from/to), the short/full address toggle (aria-expanded), and a sticky docked bar inside the .c-addr-sheet scroller.
  • Request an amount needs an onRequest callback from the host to route to Wallet › Receive and closeAddressSheet(). No bridge verb; it reuses ixian:sendrequest where it already lives.
  • Desktop dialog needs its own hero proportions; dark band contrast must be re-checked (white text on #24509e passes, on the light #3f8ff7 top it needs the scrim shown here).
  • Risk: the same smoke pins as A, plus the near-full height rule (#575/#598) and the shared sheet chrome.

Recommendation

Build A now. It answers “out of place” with the smallest change: one card in the app’s premium language, the code as the hero, identity on it, a checkable address and one obvious action. It stays inside openAddressSheet, keeps the peer fences, and needs no change to the shared sheet.

Keep B’s “start ··· end + Show all” as the fallback if the full grid ever feels heavy in long locales, and B as a later “show my code” full-screen mode if people use it to be scanned in person.

QR: payload 4JsLSmAF4yp3j1T5gcrAkiFF2fYJSe4XM7rJBngtPmPUw2kL5AXKvvwg5yYbncdFa:ixi, encoded with the app’s vendored qrcode-generator (src/components/qr.js, error correction M), version 5, 37 × 37 modules, 4-module quiet zone. Modules are always #131415 on white, in both themes, and are never tinted. Base58 has no 0, O, I or l, so the monospace face is there for column alignment, not to tell look-alike characters apart.
