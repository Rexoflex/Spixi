About and Contributors headers on desktop

DC-SCREEN-W (ADDENDUM-D §D4). The Account peer pane, rendered from the built settings shell at 1128 × 800 (a 1200-px window minus the 72-px rail; detail region 800 px). Every frame lists the bar title's contrast against the pixels really behind it: the glyphs are hidden, the ground inside the title's text box is sampled, the worst pixel decides. The pin needs 4.5 : 1 for the title and 3 : 1 for the back icon. The text-shadow is ignored. Reduced motion, so the art is a still frame.

Recommendation: option B

B uses the pane's own bar, the same as every other Account sublevel: the title spans the detail width, the content holds the 640-px column, and the ground is one flat surface (#245b, #1080 F5). The title is 16.88 : 1 in light and 15.91 : 1 in dark on both screens and both widths. The contrast comes from tokens, not from art, so a plain token pin proves it. B costs about 45 CSS lines and no new colour.

A is the phone's look and passes too (6.01 / 7.80 : 1 light, 8.90 / 11.01 : 1 dark). It needs a new top scrim, the scrollbar hidden on these two screens, a Mac title-bar check, and a pixel pin, because its contrast depends on where the art falls.
