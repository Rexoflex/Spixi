## What shipped

Two levers against the same half second, in ONE build (Damir's call — the phone reads one
number, the stamps stay separable).

**The pre-warm (#800).** A `SingleChatPage` with no friend is staged hidden in HomePage's grid
and its shell booted while the user is still on the chats list. On tap, `pushSpareChat` hands it
the friend and the same overlay path presents the stage it already holds. #796 measured what
that moves off the critical path: WebView creation (72–79 ms) + parse (102–109 ms) of a ~315 ms
median open. **Every refusal falls back to today's construct-and-stage push** — the spare is
never the only way into a conversation.

**The batch transport (#801).** `loadMessages` collects the whole load burst into one `UiBatch`
and crosses the bridge once as `addMessages(json, "append")`, then `messagesDone` ends the
shell's render burst by SIGNAL instead of by its 250 ms timer. Both transports stay live on both
ends: an old exe still paints on the timer. `position: "prepend"` is accepted and never derives
the unread boundary — the shell contract for B2 is complete; this exe sends only `append`.

**NOT built, as decisions:** B2 (prepend on load-more) and B4 (`Config.messagesToLoad`) — see
#801. ⛔ #779 (the retained warm WebView) stays parked with the lead.

**The seven defects self-review did not find**, in the order they were found:

4. The shell's 250 ms safety timer spanned the whole batch BUILD: on a long load-more it fired

   the batch — gone until re-open.
6. A throw in `updateReactions` escaped `loadMessages` before the three pushes, so **every
   history whose first row is a contact request opened EMPTY**.
7. A pin built from a **file list** instead of a walk let a `dropSpareChat` site in an unlisted
   file put a **wallet address into `ixian.log`** on every conversation open (the Opus round).

Everything else was pin coverage: one-token mutations that killed a lever silently while the

### ⚠ Two things found at the END of the session, both unmeasured, both worth a row

**(1) THE PHONE WAS HOT, and thermal throttling was never controlled for.** Damir, after the walk:
*"my phone is burning up very hot."* A throttling device cannot be measured finely, and that fits
everything odd about today's numbers: `chats-after-close drop=` spanning 1..11 across THREE captures
of identical code, 58 MB of PSS drift between two reads of one build, `present t=` from 67 to 262 ms,
and the felt "sporadic from acceptable to yanky". It was recorded as noise. It may be systematic
noise with a cause. ★ No conclusion changes — the stash A/B compared two builds on the same hot
phone, so the confound cancels there — but every ABSOLUTE number in §3 is soft, and a future capture
should read `adb shell dumpsys battery | Select-String temperature` at the start and the end.
★★ THE RULE: a perf capture on a device whose thermal state is unknown is a capture with an
uncontrolled variable in it. Say so, or control it.

**(2) A RETRY LOOP WITH NO BACKOFF — the first named candidate for heat and battery.** The Windows
log carries this continuously, nine at a time, every ~2.5 s, indefinitely:

```
Could not send message to 14cBE7… due to missing encryption keys!   (×9)
12:40:55 · 12:40:58 · 12:41:00 · 12:41:03 · 12:41:05 · 12:41:08 · 12:41:10 · 12:41:13 · 12:41:15 …
```

Nine contacts whose keys never arrive, retried forever with no backoff. On a desktop that is wasted
cycles; on a phone it is CPU plus radio wakeups, which is heat and battery. ⚠ NOT confirmed on
Android — every phone capture this session was filtered to `CDPERF`, so it could not have appeared.
Same Core, so it is likely. It predates this branch by a long way. Verify before anyone touches it
(#215): `adb logcat -c; adb logcat | Select-String "missing encryption keys"`.

⚠⚠ **TESTED THE SAME EVENING, AND THE FIRST HYPOTHESIS IS REFUTED.** A fresh empty account was
silent (consistent with every theory, so it proved nothing), and then **`Seed heavy` — 50 contacts
with NO encryption keys, ~11 600 messages — was ALSO silent.** So "keyless contacts are enough to
start the loop" is WRONG. Whatever drives it needs something the seed does not create: most likely
real contacts with genuinely pending OUTBOUND traffic (a key exchange in flight, receipts to
deliver), not merely an absent key. The loop is still real and still has no backoff — it is in the
Windows log of Damir's own account, nine lines every ~2.5 s indefinitely — but it is NOT reproduced
by contact count, and nobody should go looking for it in the keyless path.
★ The prediction was written down before the test and it failed; that is the point of writing it
down. Do not let a later reader inherit the refuted version.

### ★★ THE LADDER, FINISHED — the Account sublevels are the target, the chats list is NOT

Fresh `.dev` install, brand-new account, phone cool (32 °C start). `dumpsys gfxinfo`, one window per
routine, identical routines across rungs.

| window | rung 0 · EMPTY account | rung 2 · heavy seed (50 contacts, ~11 600 msgs) |
|---|---|---|
| **Account minute** (3× Backup/Password/Download) | 12.19 % · 23 / 30 / **48 ms** | **21.66 %** · 42 / 65 / **150 ms** |
| **Chat half** (scroll 50 rows + 3 opens) | n/a (no chats) | **4.09 %** · 8 / 13 / 57 ms · 1907 frames |
| GPU 99th | 11 ms | 10 ms |

**① The Account sublevels are the defect, and they need no data to show it.** 12.19 % janky on an
account with ZERO chats, and Damir's own read: *"it's yanky, the account backup and rest, not
smooth."* Three pages, three cold Chromium boots on the main thread — `SettingsPage.xaml.cs:383` ·
`:489` · `:501`. The GPU is idle through every janky frame at both rungs.

**② Data MAY lengthen the stalls — POSSIBLE, NOT SHOWN.** The 99th tripled (48 → 150 ms) and the
90th nearly doubled between rungs. ⚠ Two reasons not to bank it. (a) The RATE is the weak number:
absolute janky frames barely moved (39 → 34) and the percentage doubled mostly because the
denominator halved (320 → 157 frames), which depends on how briskly the tester taps. (b) ★★ THE
CONFOUND: the phone ran 32 °C at rung 0, 35 °C after the Account minute, **38 °C by the end**, and
`level` FELL from 59 to 56 while ON THE CABLE — the app plus screen plus adb draws more than the
charger supplies. Rung 2 was therefore measured on a materially warmer phone than rung 0, and this
run cannot separate 11 600 messages from 6 °C. RE-MEASURE with the rungs in REVERSE order, or with a
real cool-down between them, before anyone plans work on the strength of it (#294).

**③ The chats list is exonerated, and the "scrolling is yanky" complaint is re-scoped.** Scrolling
50 rows and opening three conversations reads **4.09 % janky, 90th 8 ms** — better than the app's
own idle baseline, with the batch transport carrying ~11 600 messages behind it. ★ Damir's original
scroll complaint was on his REAL account (live presence traffic, and the no-backoff retry loop
grinding nine lines every 2.5 s). This profile reproduces the ROW COUNT faithfully and scrolls
beautifully — so **row count is not the cause**. Whatever it is involves live network activity on a
real account, which is a far narrower target than "the app got slower".

★★ **WHY ① AND ③ SURVIVE THE THERMAL CONFOUND AND ② DOES NOT — the internal control.** The chat
half and the Account minute were taken minutes apart, on the SAME profile, at the SAME temperature
(~38 °C), and they differ FIVE-FOLD: 4.09 % / 90th 8 ms against 21.66 % / 90th 42 ms. Heat cannot
explain a gap that appears in one routine and not the other under identical thermal conditions.
Rung 0's 12.19 % was also the coolest reading of the evening, so it is if anything an
under-statement. Only the BETWEEN-RUNG comparison (②) is exposed, because only it varies
temperature along with the thing being tested.
★ THE RULE: when a confound is discovered late, do not throw the whole run away — ask which
comparisons vary with it and which do not. Same-temperature comparisons survive; across-temperature
ones do not.
