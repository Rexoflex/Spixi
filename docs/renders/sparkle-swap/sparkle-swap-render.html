Sparkle swap

Yes, it reads as Gemini. A white four-point star in a violet rounded tile is the Gemini mark and the generic “AI” sign. The same star is drawn in 28 drawings of the set, about 90 times, plus the Contributors tap burst. Below: replacements drawn into the app's own scenes (the real art from src/components/illustrations*.js; only the sparkle changes).

Recommendation

    1 · The glyph in the purple tile

    The strong Gemini look. Four candidates for “mini apps / something new to discover”, in the four tile scenes: Apps empty state (Damir's screenshot), Welcome 4, Welcome 3 (the violet disc) and the Explore banner. The small companion star goes too; the glyph sits in the tile centre. Twinkles in this section use the recommended decoration.

    2 · The small twinkles

    Weaker on their own, but they repeat the same star about 80 times across the set, so the AI cue stays even after the tile glyph goes. Three candidates, in the Apps scene, the What's new “mini apps” card, the Contributors heart and the AI-translation moment. The tile glyph in this section is the recommended one.

    3 · The star on the gold coin

    Six scenes put the same star on the IXI coin (Backup, both Wallet empty states, About header, the First IXI and Pay moments). On gold it reads less as Gemini, but it is the same mark. A tile glyph makes no sense on a coin, so the coin gets its own face.

    4 · Every scene that draws the sparkle

    All 28 drawings, light and dark, at in-app size. Pick any combination; “Now” shows the shipped art. Default = the recommendation.

    5 · Where the sparkle lives

    Content = a glyph inside a tile or on a coin (the strong look). Decorative = small background twinkles. One path, M0-6.5C.5-2.2 2.2-.5 6.5 0 …Z, defined once per drawing as <prefix>Sp@@ / …sp@@ and placed with <use>.

    6 · Where the sparkle meant something

    In most scenes it is only light. In these it carried a meaning, and something else must carry it now.

    Built by docs/renders/sparkle-swap/build-sparkle-swap.mjs from the shipped art (src/components/illustrations.js, illustrations-s17.js), motion (src/styles/components/illo.css) and theme values (src/styles/tokens.css). The swap is a string edit on each drawing's own sparkle <use>s, so a port into src/ touches only the …Sp@@ defs and the content <use>s. Motion classes (il-tw, mo-tw, rn-tw, ct-tw) are kept, so the twinkle timing is unchanged.
