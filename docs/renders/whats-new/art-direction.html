Spixi · art direction · round 1

Three ways to give Spixi a face

Three illustration and motion languages to replace the glossy tiles. Each one grows from the same place, the Spixi mark, and each one is drawn here in the What’s new spotlight, in light and dark, with real copy.

The insight

The logo already tells the story.

A circle and a speech bubble overlap. The lens between them is the only place they share, and two dots sit inside it. Two people, and the private space between them. No server, no audience.

So every direction below keeps that grammar: two shapes, one shared lens, two dots. It makes the art unmistakably Spixi, and it turns the product promise into a picture.

Monzo design notes
Art theme
01

Lens · glass and light

The world is frosted glass. Only where the two shapes overlap is it sharp: you can read it only together. Light pools under the lens, a glint runs the rim.

Swipe, tap the dots or Next

Material

Frosted glass over a living aurora; specular rims lit from the top left; a pink / cyan chromatic fringe only on the lens edge.

Depth

Three planes: aurora · glass · floating glass pebbles in front. A caustic of focused light on the ground.

Motion

Glass slides in, the lens comes into focus (1 s), a glint sweeps the rim every 6 s, the caustic breathes.

Palette

The launch aurora: violet, sapphire, magenta, cyan.

02

Press · two inks, one overlap

Risograph print: a pink and a blue ink. Where the two shapes overlap, the inks print a third colour by themselves, the Spixi violet. Grain, halftone, stamps that land in four frames.

Swipe, tap the dots or Next

Material

Cream paper (ink-black paper in dark), two inks with rough edges, halftone shading, a registration mark.

Depth

Overprint instead of shadow: layers stack as ink, multiply in light and screen in dark.

Motion

Stop-motion: stamps land in 4 steps, hand-drawn lines “boil” at 8 fps. No easing curves at all.

Palette

Riso blue (≈ brand indigo), fluorescent pink, and the violet they make together.

03

Spark · the mark is alive

The two dots are eyes. The logo drops in, squashes, blinks and looks at you (move your pointer). It hums for voice, closes its eyes for privacy, and winks.

Move the pointer over the page

Material

Soft clay tubes with a gloss line, a lit face in the lens, a touch of blush. Friendly, never cute-overload.

Depth

Character · floating chips · glow and contact shadow. The character always stands on the ground.

Motion

Animation principles: anticipation, squash and stretch, overshoot. The eyes follow the finger and the swipe.

Palette

Brand indigo tubes, a near-white face, ink eyes, pink blush only.

My recommendation, as director

Spark for personality, lit with Lens. The character is the logo itself: it needs no mascot introduction, it carries emotion (blink, wink, hum, close the eyes for privacy), and it scales to every empty state and nudge for nothing. Use Lens light (the aurora, the rim glint, the caustic) only on the big moments: the hero card of What's new, the launch flow and the backup success. Press is the bravest of the three, and I would keep it for marketing (store screenshots, the website, social), where a second ink is an asset, not a governance cost.

If you pick

  1. One expression sheet for the chosen direction (idle · look · blink · happy · calm · wink · surprised · sleepy), light + dark.
  2. The six What's new scenes in it, inside the spotlight card, with the fit check.
  3. Then the 14 existing drawings, in the order: welcome 1–4 → empty states → backup / rating.
