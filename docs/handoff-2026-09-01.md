## ★★ What the loop found, and why it matters more than the rows

Three auditors, disjoint scopes. **4 MAJOR. Two of them were mine and no gate could see
either.**

1. **L10 was a no-op that would have reported itself as a success.**
   `MainThread.BeginInvokeOnMainThread` runs INLINE when already on the main thread — the
   tree says so in its own code — so the present completed synchronously and the burst never
   left the turn. And `[CDPERF] presented` fires either way, so a device run would have
   printed a ~140 ms win over an unchanged screen. It posts for real now, **and it ships its
   own discriminator**: `roster burst` must be the LARGER number, or the fix is not working.
2. **L6's rail fix was defeated one line later** by an unconditional `setNavActive` in
   `onSettingsClosed` whose comment called it "a consistent no-op". The rail had four
   writers. The repair was to **delete the disagreement**, not add a fifth.
3. Dropping `ixian:tab:` dropped **`repaintOwnSystemBars()`** with it — and closing that
   also closed a **pre-existing** hole on the wallet's own Receive/Send takeover.
4. **The flags failed at real size**, and only a rasteriser could say so — and then
   **Damir deleted the whole class**: the emoji is perfect where it works, so it STAYS,
   and only the platforms that cannot paint one get a fallback. The fallback was already
   in the repo (`img/flags/*.png`, shipping since legacy, referenced by nothing). Three
   rounds of my artwork, gone to one line of his. Same shape as #658.
