### L13 · the leave-group check box (#676) — ★ and the row's own description was half wrong

The handoff called it *"fully traced, nothing left to discover"*: add a twin verb *"calling
the same `sendLeave` + `FriendList.removeFriend`"*. **Two of the three pieces already
existed.** `SContacts.leaveGroup(Friend)` IS that body and has been since #567
(`SContacts.cs:68`); `ixian:removecontact:<addr>:<flag>` ALREADY routes a room into it and
answers `"left"` (`:102`). What was missing was the **shell** half — `openDeleteFlow` builds
its third box as `isGroupChat ? null : removeContact`, so a room got **two** boxes and one
verb, `ixian:removehistory:`. Deleting a group chat destroyed your copy and left you in the
group, receiving it, with the row back on the next message.

Built: `ixian:leavegroup:<addr>` → `onLeaveGroupFor` → **`SContacts.leaveGroup`, called not
re-inlined**; the room's third box (**OFF by default**) with a note; **ONE verb, not two**;
`leaveGroupResult` with a `"left" | "fail"` vocabulary; the open conversation resolved
BEFORE the leave and popped after.

★ **Why one verb.** Read at Core `097341a`: `FriendList.removeFriend` deletes the history
file and the avatar *before* it drops the record (`FriendList.cs:436-440`), so the leave
satisfies the fixed "Delete chat" box on its own. Sending both would race, and the loser
would answer `fail` about a friend the winner had already removed — **un-tombstoning a row
that is correctly gone.**

★ **What the box may truthfully promise** (Core `097341a`): the leave is fanned out **per
member** (`sendGroupSpixiMessage` → `sendSpixiMessage(pf, …)` for each member's own Friend)
with `add_to_pending_messages` and `send_push_notification` both true — so it reaches
members who are offline, **and the pending copies live on the MEMBER contacts, not on the
group record**, which is why removing the group immediately afterwards cannot strand them.
On their device `handleLeave` runs `users.delUser(sender)`; an OWNER leaving runs
`FriendList.removeFriend(group)` and the group is gone for everyone.
