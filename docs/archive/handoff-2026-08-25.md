## 2. ★ The batch, in the order it matters

### #493 · #494 · #495 — notifications when the app is CLOSED

Damir: *"if I close the app the notifications keep coming and they are not grouped, so like
legacy"*. The handler that filters and groups them registered inside `Node.start()`, ~4
seconds after launch; a push to a killed app is shown by OneSignal's native code long before
that. It registers in the **Application** now. The cold push is also re-posted as **our own
row keyed on the sender**, so it collapses instead of stacking — and that reads **no new
OneSignal API**, on purpose, because nothing here can compile.

⚠ **Honest scope, and it is in the F5 sheet at 2.6:** the **global master** works from the
first millisecond (a plain preference). The **per-1:1 mute** does not — it resolves through
`FriendList`, which a cold process has not loaded, and it fails **open**. The one-line fix
was considered and **refused**: `fa` on a group push is the SENDER'S address, so it would
silently drop group messages from anyone you muted 1:1.

### #496 + #500 — the lock grace window. ★ READ #500.

Damir's call: the window measures from **backgrounding**, not from the last unlock. Then the
audit found that the first cut **opened the app with no password** — the stamp was cleared
even when the lock stayed up, so pressing Home and coming back within five seconds, or
simply **cancelling the pattern prompt** (it is a separate Activity, so presenting it pauses
Spixi and lays a fresh stamp), dismissed an unauthenticated lock after hours away.

Fixed, pinned both ways, written up in `docs/security-handover-gate.md`, and the two
sequences are now sheet rows **§3.6** and **§3.7**. **Walk them.**

### iOS-62 · iOS-61 · iOS-56 · N83 — the small ones

The long-press tint (his own decision, #492) · the empty-state art warmed at idle · the lock
switch that **lied after a cancelled confirm** — and iOS-56 is worth reading, because the
report rested on a wrong premise and pointed at a real defect one layer down: Cancel is
correct on that screen; what was broken was the switch reading OFF while the lock was still
ON, a gap `settings.html` had filed as "BE gap S6" and which was ours all along.

### #497 + #502 — the sounds, and their credit

UI SFX `zen`, CC0, picked by measurement across all twelve packs and re-gained to −12 dBFS.
Credited on the Contributors screen in its own list — not folded in with the people.

### #501 — iOS-59 was built and REVERTED

The wallet hero fix assumed the hero sits inside the scroller. It is a **sibling**, and the
scroller is `flex: 1` — so collapsing the hero *enlarges* the viewport, the opposite sign.
It could not have worked with one row and would have juddered with two. Reverted; the
**measurement** ships instead, on the dev HUD. Two readings from Damir solve it properly.

## 5. ★ What this batch taught

* ★ **The audit is not a formality.** #500 shipped an authentication gate weaker than the
  design it replaced, in the one place Damir explicitly handed over as his call. No pin, no
  amount of reading and no amount of writing about lifecycle edges caught it — only somebody
  trying to break it.
* ★ **MAUI lifecycle edges, the fifth time.** #442, #454, #460, #472, and now #500. The new
  one: a device-credential prompt is a separate **Activity**, so putting it on screen pauses
  the app that asked for it.
* ★ **"Never build past a missing repro" has a second half — do not guess the GEOMETRY.**
  #501's fix was written, pinned four ways, and every pin stayed green with the feature
  turned off, because the harness never executes that path. A model of the DOM is a
  hypothesis; the device is the test.
* ★ **A pin can count its own subject's declaration** (#494), **test a function nothing
  calls** (#495, and again at iOS-61), **match the change's own comment** (#496's log pin),
  or **assert one thing while testing another** (iOS-56). Five of those were found by
  mutation, not by reading.
* ★ **Verify the premise, not just the report.** iOS-56 and N65 both arrived as one
  complaint and turned out to be a different, real defect one layer down. Both were found by
  checking whether the reported thing was actually wrong.
* ★ **A fix that cannot be compiled should be designed not to need compiling.** #495 posts
  the app's own string rather than the push payload, which is better for privacy AND removes
  the only unverifiable API in the batch.
