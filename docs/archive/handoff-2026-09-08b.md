## ★★ What the walk's free row found, and why it matters more than the six strings

Damir: the Account address line, the peer address sheet in contact info, and the
remove-contact sheet are English under a chosen locale.

**Exactly six keys are the English string in ALL TWELVE locales:**

```
spixiAddressSub · peerAddressTitle · peerAddressBody
peerQrLabel     · removeContactOpt · removeFailedToast
```

Their immediate neighbours — `removeContactTitle`, `removeContactBody`, `keepContact`,
`copyAddress`, `shareAddress` — are all translated. That is the tell: these six were added
**after the last drafting round**, they are **absent from every `src/strings/draft/*.json`**,
and `build-locales` falls back to English. Silently. In twelve languages at once.

⚠ **No gate catches it, and the obvious gate cannot.** `verify-locales` reports
"still-English N" as information, and it must — most of that count is legitimate ("Apps",
"Wallet", "Status" are the same word in German).

★ **The unambiguous signal is the one nobody tests: a key that is English in ALL twelve AND
missing from every draft file.** Nine keys meet the first half today — two are correctly
untranslatable (`appUrlPlaceholder`, `gif`), one is a phantom (below), six are the defect.
**That predicate is the pin the next session owes**, and it is worth more than the six
strings, because the next key added after a drafting round will do this again.

⚠ **A phantom key ships in all 13 dictionaries:** `someKey: "fallback"` — the EXAMPLE from a
docblock in `settings-app.js` (`strings.someKey || 'fallback'`) that `extract-strings`
scraped as a real reference. Nothing renders it, but it is in the bundle, and it proves the
sweep cannot tell a doc example from a call site.

**And a correction on the record:** #812 said German had "33 still-English keys". Twenty-one
of those are correct. The real gap is **6**, and it is the same 6 everywhere. The translation
pass is much smaller than that row implied.
