A · Conversation

A1
Typed cards carry the lift

A call card, a payment card, an app invite and a file bubble each show the same faint shadow the text bubbles have — light and dark. Sent cards too (blue border + lift).

A2
⊕ pressed = the 36 disc

Hold the ⊕: the tint is a small disc inside the pill, not a big one spilling past its edge. With the tray open the ✕ tint is the same disc.

A3
Caret blinks on entry, no keyboard (Android)

Open a chat: a blue caret blinks in the field and NO keyboard rises. If it does not blink → F, and the dial is dropped next session (one constant).

A4
First tap on the field: keyboard rises, no dip

Fresh chat → tap ⊕ (tray) → tap the field. The tray leaves as the keyboard arrives; the bar does NOT drop and rise again. This is the exact order that flickered before.

A5
Keyboard ↔ tray swap: same height, no move

Keyboard up → ⊕: the tray takes the slot at once and is the SAME height as the keyboard was (after the first keyboard of the session; the bar does not move). Tap the field: keyboard back, tray gone in one frame. Repeat twice — the second time is the one that counts.

A6
Selected message tick

Long-press → Select: the tick sits centred in the disc, white; in dark the disc is one shade deeper than the action blue.

A7
Light canvas — your values

Ground #E4EAF3, pattern ink #051C8E at 6%. Account → Chat appearance → Canvas: the two swatches differ; Gradient = your 289° teal → periwinkle wash on the real chat.

A8

White card with a lift in light, midnight blue with a lift in dark, no border — it no longer melts into the ground.

A9
