# The three, side by side

| | Lens | Press | Spark |
|---|---|---|---|
| The feeling | premium, calm, a little magic | human, crafted, collectable | warm, funny, alive |
| Built from the mark | the overlap is the only clear place | the overlap prints its own colour | the two dots are eyes |
| Material | liquid glass, specular rims, a chromatic fringe, a caustic | two riso inks, overprint, halftone, paper grain | soft clay tubes with gloss, a lit face, blush |
| Signature motion | the lens comes into focus; a glint runs the rim; light pools under it | stamps land in 4 frames; lines "boil" at 8 fps | drop, squash, blink, look at you, hum, wink |
| Light and dark | both, from tokens (aurora tints) | cream paper and ink-black paper (multiply ↔ screen) | both, from tokens |
| Next to the indigo UI | the closest fit; extends the launch aurora | the boldest contrast; it brings a new pink | fits; the character IS the logo |
| Against incumbents | Apple-grade polish; nobody in messaging owns "glass you can only see through together" | nobody in finance or messaging prints; very memorable | Duolingo-level personality without a separate mascot to maintain |
| Risk | can drift into "another glass trend" | can read as playful for a wallet; a second brand colour to govern | overuse; needs an expression guide and taste |
| WebView cost | highest: 3–4 copies of the aurora per glass, static blur filters; drift off on low-end devices | low after one change: the grain filter becomes a pre-rendered noise tile; stop-motion is cheap | lowest: gradients, strokes, transforms; ~20 lines of JS for the eyes |
| Effort to adopt | ~2 sessions (system + the 14 drawings) | ~2 sessions | ~1.5 sessions + an expression sheet (8 expressions) |

## My recommendation, as director

**Spark for personality, lit with Lens.** The character is the logo itself: it needs no mascot introduction, it carries emotion (blink, wink, hum, close the eyes for privacy), and it scales to every empty state and nudge for nothing. Use Lens light (the aurora, the rim glint, the caustic) only on the big moments: the hero card of What's new, the launch flow and the backup success. Press is the bravest of the three, and I would keep it for marketing (store screenshots, the website, social), where a second ink is an asset, not a governance cost.

## If you pick

1. One expression sheet for the chosen direction (idle · look · blink · happy · calm · wink · surprised · sleepy), light + dark.
2. The six What's new scenes in it, inside the spotlight card, with the fit check.
3. Then the 14 existing drawings, in the order: welcome 1–4 → empty states → backup / rating.
