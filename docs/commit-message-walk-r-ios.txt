Walk R on the iPhone — four dead gates, the iOS launcher icon, and 35/2 (#807–#810)

The first iOS device pass since 2026-08-27. It covered Sessions I → R, six sessions
that had never been on this platform, and the app came out well: 35 pass, 2 fail,
4 n/a. Chat open is fast and the bot group that froze the app for minutes in August
loads normally.

What it found instead was FOUR DEAD GATES, none of them in the app, all the same
shape: the suite pinned what a thing DECLARES and never that it WORKS.

  W-R6  strip-release.mjs:117 — its main-module guard compared a REALPATH against an
        as-typed path. On macOS /var is a symlink to /private/var, which is where
        smoke-packaged always puts its copy, so the CLI was skipped and the process
        exited 0 having written nothing. GATE 2 HAS NEVER WORKED ON A MAC.
  W-R7  smoke-packaged.mjs excluded local-nuget/ from the package, so gate 22 — added
        in Session R — could never find the .nupkg it hashes. Symlinked back in; the
        exclusion's "hundreds of MB" reason is true of obj/ and bin/, not of 1 MB.
  W-R8  E1c's own SVG broke the build with MAUIR0001: an XML comment cannot contain
        '--'. No pin had ever opened the artwork.
  W-R9  The resizetizer names the .appiconset after the MauiIcon FILE, and each
        Info.plist names the set it wants as a hardcoded string. No pin had ever
        opened an Info.plist.

E1c gives iOS its own launcher icon. It is E1b's measured argument one platform over:
iOS neither masks with headroom nor zooms the foreground, so the Android adaptive
padding rendered the mark at about two thirds size. appicon_ios.svg is full-bleed and
opaque with no rounding of its own, the ground copied verbatim from appicon.svg and
the mark copied verbatim from appiconfg.svg at 1.6x about the tile centre — about 62%
of the tile. Verified at the generated artwork, never at the springboard.

Every new pin was mutation-tested red. Counts: committed 4621, packaged 4620.

STILL OWED, and it is the lesson: nothing in the suite asserts that a GATE CAN
EXECUTE. Four gates were green by silence.
