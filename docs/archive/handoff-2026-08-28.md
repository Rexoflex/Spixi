## 2 · ★★ The two findings worth reading even if you skip the rest

**The bot group.** You said it worked for the first few weeks and one session broke it,
that it does not need core, and that legacy is fine. All three were right.
`hideParticipantAddresses` is a GROUP privacy mode, and legacy qualifies **every** mask on
`friend.type == FriendType.Group` — a bot room could not be masked whatever the wire said.
Two of our commits lost that qualifier: **`99e2106b` (#348, 15 Aug)** passed the raw flag
to the chat shell, and **`55077199` (#248, 10 Jul)** read it unqualified in
`ContactDetails`, the surface that renders all group and bot info since #249. Fixed with
one shared predicate plus an independent belt in the shell. ⚠ The **money path is
deliberately excluded** — a blind group pays a derived address and nothing in the tree
says which a flagged bot room hands out, so the tip refusal still reads the raw flag
(#215). A blind private group is still masked; a public channel is not.

**The lifted row, fourth round.** The three earlier rounds all raised `z-index` and all
three were measured on Android. The row lives inside `#chat-scroll`, which on iOS is a
real composited scroller (WKWebView's own scroll view is disabled, so every `.u-scroll`
is one); whether WebKit will hoist a descendant out of that layer to clear a sibling of it
is the question all three rounds assumed an answer to. This one does not ask it.

## 4b · ★★ An adversarial pass ran before this left the machine, and it found three defects

I pointed two independent auditors at my own batch with one instruction: break it. They
did, and the fixes are in the tree (DECISIONS #616). Recording it here because two of the
three were mine and one was older than the batch:

* **MAJOR — the address sheet opened OFF-SCREEN with the keyboard up.** `wallet-receive.css`
  loads after `overlay.css` at the same specificity, so its bare `max-height: 100%` beat
  the new keyboard-aware cap while `bottom: var(--kb-inset)` still applied: the sheet was
  lifted and never shortened, putting its handle, title, only Close button and the QR
  above the top of the screen, unscrollable. Three taps to reach. **Worse than the defect
  #608 was fixing.**
* **HIGH — #613 re-created the dead Tip button** its own 7th argument exists to prevent.
  ★ The fix is the lesson: one wire value, two named questions — `hidesAddresses` (raw:
  can these people be PAID or ADDED) and `blind` (group-only: must they be MASKED).
* **HIGH — #613 lit up a "Contact request sent" toast for something never sent**, because
  C# refuses that action for `friend.bot` outright. ⚠ **This was reachable on every
  non-blind bot room before this batch existed** — the review found a pre-existing defect
  while auditing a new one.

Plus six MEDIUMs, all fixed: the owner chip re-gated on `type == Group`; #612 no longer
burns its budget during the lock (the loop ticks every **2** seconds, not one, so the
first cut spent ~20s there and dropped the link before the lock closed — the very symptom
it fixes), no longer swallows the whole update tick, and is keyed to its address; #609
acts on the lift with a movement slop, because `pointerdown` cannot tell a tap from the
start of a scroll — and #608's own reserved space is what the user scrolls; #610's
ellipsis was **inert** on a flex container and moved to `.c-chip__label`; #606's ghost now
follows a re-render, resolves the live row after a flush, and carries an identity token.

**29 of 29 mutations caught across the batch.**

⚠ Two dials this surfaced, both yours:
* **The tip chips are no longer equal width** in English (measured 74/74/86/103 against a
  uniform 84.3 before). Wrapping is what the verdict asked for and clipping is gone, but
  the equal-width intent stated in that file is now a deviation. One declaration to
  reverse; the label ellipsis then carries the German case instead.
* **#611**: `shouldNotify` returns false the moment the GLOBAL notifications master is
  off, while the ring deliberately ignores it. So a user who has silenced banners now
  hears a call ring, misses it, and gets no missed-call row at all. The per-contact mute
  half is exactly what you asked for; this edge is worth a word.
