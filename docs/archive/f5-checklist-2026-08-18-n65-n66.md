## A — N66, the OS-follow theme path

| # | Leg | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Windows: app open, appearance = System. Flip the OS theme (Settings → Personalisation → Colours). | The app follows within a moment. **Before this batch nothing happened at all.** |
| A2 | Same flip, look at the panes together: chats list + open conversation + the welcome pane. | All three agree. No pane keeps the old theme |
| A3 | Android: appearance = System, flip the OS theme. | The whole app follows |

| A8 | ★ **Regression leg (review MAJOR-1).** Open a mini-app, let it run, then flip the OS theme. | The mini-app **keeps running and keeps its state**. It must NOT restart from its entry point. (It also does not change theme — that is correct, it is third-party content) |
