What Session R did, in one paragraph

The security handover gate finally ran — eight disjoint auditors over the whole delta from the fork point, then two adversarial verifiers. 82 filed rows became 65 introduced; 38 are fixed and 27 stay open. Three MAJORs mattered here more than anywhere: the media auto-fetch had lost its host constraint and iOS was one of only two platforms where that leak was live; account restore carried a zip-slip that ran before the wallet check; and iOS granted camera and microphone to mini-app content with no prompt at all. Then six rounds of the #46 loop rebuilt the link-open gate three times, and found that the suite comment-stripper had been hiding 17 live lines from every negative sweep.

CLEAN is not claimed. Round 6 fixed two MAJORs and re-ran their reproductions red, but no seventh round reviewed that fix. Also: cs-syntax-check proves braces, not types — your build was the first compile of a batch that touched a lot of C#.

R · The Release build itself

Release runs the packaging strip target (Session N, #791–#795). The MSBuild leg of that target has never been verified anywhere but on a Windows deploy, so gate 1 against this app bundle is real work, not a formality.
