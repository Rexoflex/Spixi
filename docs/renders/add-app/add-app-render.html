Today shipped

A faithful redraw of createAppsAdd({ discover: false }) at 360 px. The keyboard is not drawn in the field states.

What is wrong

  • A broken promise in line one. “Or explore the directory below” points at nothing, in every state, on every platform (both callers pass discover: false).
  • The main path has a door in front of it. Paste link opens a hidden field, then you paste, then Get app. The field appears in the middle of the screen and pushes the grey box down.
  • Three equal tiles say three equal ways. A shared link is the common case; From file (a .spixi package on disk) is mostly a developer path.
  • Grey on grey. Grey discs and a grey info box: next to the redesigned About, Downloads and Contributors screens this reads as a stub, and the safety line looks like small print to skip.

What is not wrong

  • The empty lower half is not the problem by itself. On a phone that space is where the keyboard goes once the link field has focus.
  • The wiring is sound: reason-specific errors, the loading spinner, the scan → fill path and the 20 s grace timer all stay as they are in both directions.

A · Link first Recommended

The link field is the screen. The other ways become rows, the directory becomes an honest link out, the safety line becomes a caption.

What changes and why

  • The field is on the screen, first, in a card. Tap it and paste; the keyboard’s clipboard chip already offers the copied link. One tap less, and nothing moves when you start.
  • Scan QR and From file become rows with the Account hub discs (violet, pink) and chevrons: the same grammar as Settings, and clearly “other ways”.
  • The directory promise is kept honestly: a third row “Explore Spixi Mini Apps” with the external-link glyph (#710: it opens outside the app) sends the existing ixian:spixiAppsLink. No in-app catalog, so A2 holds.
  • The safety line sits under the link card as a caption with the shield-lock glyph, next to the thing it warns about. No grey box.
  • No lead. The card label and the caption say it all. The free space under the cards is the keyboard’s space.

Build cost: about 3 to 4 h

  • apps-add.js createAppsAdd: field visible and wrapped in a card; the three tiles become two rows, plus the Explore row when onBrowseWeb is given (the parameter already exists); the info box becomes the caption; the lead goes. setAddUrl, setAddError and the pinned selector .c-apps-add__field .c-button keep working.
  • apps-add.css: the tile and info rules are replaced (about 70 lines). Reuses .c-disc with --disc-hub-7/8/10, --surface-settings-card, --elevation-settings-card, --surface-input-on-settings-card, ground --surface-settings-ground.
  • home.html openAppsAdd: one line, onBrowseWeb: () => bridge.send('ixian:spixiAppsLink'). app_new.html unchanged: AppNewPage has no handler for that verb, so the row is absent in the desktop pane (the Apps list with its banner is beside it there).
  • No new verb, no new string, no C#. addLead and pasteLink become unused. Two new pins (field visible at mount; no Explore row without the callback). Pipeline: build-demo-bundle → build-shells.

B · Keep the tiles, premium pass Alternative

The three-tile model you approved in the Apps rounds (#123 / #128) stays. It gets the card and disc language of the redesigned screens and an honest end.

What changes and why

  • White tiles with hub discs (blue link, violet QR, pink file) on the settings ground, in place of outlined tiles with grey discs.
  • The open tile shows it is open: a 2 px action ring and an action-coloured label, so the field card under it reads as its child.
  • The safety line becomes the shield caption. No grey box.
  • The lead loses its broken half. The promise is kept by the Apps tab’s own Explore banner, pinned to the bottom (same art, now the rocket and bokeh dots, theme-stable blue).
  • Trade-offs: the main path still costs the extra tap; the banner repeats the one on the Apps tab the user just left; the banner repeats the Apps tab.

Build cost: about 4 to 5 h

  • apps-add.css: tile and info restyle (about 50 lines), same tokens as A.
  • apps-add.js: aria-expanded on the Paste link tile, the banner mount. apps-header.js: move the banner (lines 50–95) into an exported createExploreBanner() so both screens use one builder.
  • home.html: the same one-line onBrowseWeb. app_new.html: no banner (no handler).
  • addLead trimmed in all 13 locale files → the FULL strings pipeline (extract-strings → build-locales → build-strings-iife → build-demo-bundle → build-shells).

If you leave it: change only the lead

addLead → “Add a mini app from a link, a QR code, or a file.” in en-us and the 12 other locales (cut the second sentence). About 30 minutes plus the full strings pipeline. Nothing else on the screen is broken; the rest is the design gap described above.
