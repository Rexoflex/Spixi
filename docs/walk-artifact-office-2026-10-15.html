Set-up (before phase 1)

  • Peers: the Android phone and the Windows PC on the S20G build (or the V1A-M build if Drop 2 is committed). The Android peer has ≥ 1 IXI to send.
  • Developer mode ON on the iPhone and the Mac (Account › Developer). Add the Japanese (Kana) keyboard on the iPhone.
  • Test data: a 2–3 MB PDF on the peer · a link to a mini app the iPhone / Mac does not have · a blind group · a contact with a very long name and one with no name · a chat with large images · a wallet with 50+ rows · 20+ downloads.
  • Quit and start Spixi (many rows say this). Mac — quit: press Cmd+Q. Mac — start: the same way as in OW-B1: Run in your IDE, or double-click Spixi.app in the build folder, or click its Dock icon. iPhone — quit ("close fully"): swipe up from the bottom edge and hold, then swipe the Spixi card up and off the screen. iPhone — start: tap the Spixi icon on the Home Screen.
  • No logs needed. Every row is checked in the app. A few rows say OPTIONAL — they need Terminal; skip them if you like (tap N/A).
  • A row's ID points to its full text in docs/office-walk-sheet-v1.md (§7–§15; the new OW-* rows are in §16).

★ Copy the data first (L149)
