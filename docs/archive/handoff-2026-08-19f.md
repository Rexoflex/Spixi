## 2. ★ What I could NOT verify without a device — read this before trusting anything above

Honest gaps, not hedges. Each one is a thing the F5 decides.

1. **Nothing was compiled.** Nine C# files changed. The auditors checked
   namespaces, accessibility, `using`s and `#if` placement by hand and found no
   compile fault, but that is not a compiler.
2. **The real Android top inset is unknown to me.** AND-7 publishes
   `sysInsets.Top / density` and a bootstrap estimate from `status_bar_height`.
   Both numbers, and what `env()` reports beside them, are printed on screen by
   the probe (F5 checklist §6). If a screen looks wrong, that line says which of
   the three is at fault — do not start guessing before reading it.
3. **F-2 may still be unfixed.** The report is now structural, and the C# side is
   instrumented, but I could not observe the device. The two log lines
   (`LaunchPage verb:` / `LaunchPage back:`) separate "the report never lands"
   from "the handler never runs" in one F5.
4. **F-5 is measured, not fixed** — deliberately. Two candidate mechanisms; the
   BOOT marks in the probe say which. My reading of the source favours "the
   burst is late" (the ready verb fires on window `load`, which waits for fonts
   and illustrations), but changing that timing blind would undo #177.
5. **The in-call strip on Android** is the highest-risk single item in the batch.
   The audit caught that the CSS grew and the native stage did not; the fix is
   one `#elif ANDROID`. It has never run. F5 §1.6.
6. **The locale drafts want a native eye**, especially `tipNoAnswer` (the longest
   line, and the only one carrying a hedge) and the ru/sl/sr count phrasings.
   The mechanical half is proven by the gates; idiom is not.
7. **AND-7 residual, logged not hidden:** the inset is baked into the document at
   `generatePage` time and re-pushed on every page-chrome pass, so a rotation
   while a page is ALREADY on screen keeps the old value until the next visit. A
   live push needs a page enumerator — the #284 "every reload-them-all helper has
   missed a collection" class — so it was not written blind. Mostly invisible;
   visible on a CUTOUT device rotated to landscape.
8. **Windows and iOS are untested for this batch.** AND-7 is a no-op on both by
   construction (`--safe-top` collapses to `env()`), and the one iOS-visible
   change is the mini-app safe-area restore (security MAJOR #6(b), now fixed).

  batch shipped three self-defeating pins that matched their own explanatory
  comments, one security pin that was `A || B` with A unsatisfiable, and two
