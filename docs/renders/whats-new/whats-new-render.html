3 · Motion — the "kick"

LayerRule
Card 1 = full bleedthe animated aurora is the card's own ground; title, body, dots and button sit on it in white
The hand-offwhile the finger drags 1 → 2, the aurora collapses with the finger from the whole card into the panel band (clip-path, follows the finger 1:1), and card 2 arrives with its own full-bleed scene in that band
Depth (parallax)each scene has a background and a foreground layer; while the finger drags, the background moves at −0.42 × and the foreground at +0.16 × the card offset, so the scene has depth; the ends rubber-band
Entranceeach scene plays once, the first time its card shows (il-* + wn-* classes), then holds — the launch rule (#1263 "one entrance per lifetime")
Loopsbackgrounds only, ≤ 0.5 Hz, transform / opacity; a card that is not on screen pauses its loops
Reduced motionno animation, no parallax, every piece at its END frame (switches off, pin struck, heart placed)
Costinline SVG, no filter on a moving layer (blobs are radial gradients), no bitmap; ~118 KB raw for all six scenes (each repeats its gradient defs; one shared defs block halves it), built only when its card first shows
