## 1. ★★ The one thing to read before writing any code

**#493 registered the OneSignal handler in the Application. It works, and it does nothing
for the case it was written for.** `WillDisplay` is a **FOREGROUND lifecycle listener**;
background and killed-app notifications are drawn by the OneSignal SDK in native code, on a
path our own manifest has styled by name since #334. The registration is verified in the
log, `Cannot clear notifications` is gone — and no gate ever runs for a cold push.

**The fix is a `NotificationServiceExtension`, on BOTH platforms.** See #503. Own batch,
own audit. Do not re-aim at `WillDisplay`.

⚠ And **2.1 was a FALSE PASS** — the "4" badge was Samsung's bundler, not our row. When a
notification row is the subject, read the screenshot, not the tick.

## 5. What this round taught

* ★ **A fix can be correct, pinned, mutation-tested — and aimed at the wrong layer.** Every
  gate was green and the lane did nothing. Mechanism is a thing to verify, like a repro.
* ★ **The answer was in a comment we wrote ourselves** (#334, the SDK-rendered background
  path). Re-read the surface before adding to it.
* ★ **A PASS can be false.** 2.1 was the platform's bundler wearing our result.
* ★ **Nine batches without a device IS the defect.** N65 was solvable the day #385 shipped.
* ★ **A dial copied from mobile to desktop measures a different thing.** "Backgrounded"
  means the phone left your hand; on a desktop it means you clicked a browser.
* ★ **The desk measurement lost to the device again** — the sound pack (#502) and the tint
  (#506②). Numbers are a hypothesis; the device is the test. Third time (#433).
